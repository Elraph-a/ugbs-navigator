### Finding the right support service

Students describe a problem in ordinary language and are often unsure which office owns
it. This guide maps common situations to the unit that handles them. When in doubt, the
Dean of Student Affairs is the general entry point and will refer onward.

### Personal, emotional or mental health difficulty

The University Counselling Centre provides confidential counselling to registered
students. Appointments can be made in person, and urgent cases are seen sooner. A student
whose difficulty is affecting academic work should also tell their academic advisor, who
can note it before examinations rather than afterwards, when far less can be done.

### Accommodation problems

Matters inside a hall of residence — room allocation, maintenance, roommate disputes,
safety — are handled first by the Hall Administration or the Senior Tutor of the hall.
Disputes with a private hostel outside the University are not University matters, but the
Dean of Student Affairs can advise.

### Financial hardship

A student unable to meet fees should approach the Students Financial Aid Office before the
registration deadline rather than after it. Discuss a payment arrangement with the Finance
Directorate in writing; an informal understanding is not a record. Where hardship is
temporary, deferment may preserve the place on the programme.

### Illness and medical matters

The University Hospital serves registered students. A medical report from the Hospital, or
from a recognised facility, is what supporting documentation means in deferment and
examination-absence applications.

### Harassment, discrimination or misconduct

Report to the Dean of Student Affairs. Serious allegations are handled through the
University's disciplinary procedures rather than by an individual department, and a student
may bring a representative.

### Academic difficulty

Speak to your academic advisor or Head of Department. Where the problem is a specific
grade, use the change of grade process through the teaching department; where it is
progression on the programme, the department advises on the options.
