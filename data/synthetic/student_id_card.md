### Student identity card

The student identity card proves studentship. It is required to enter examination halls,
borrow from the University libraries, collect documents such as transcripts, and access
hall facilities.

### Getting your card as a fresh student

1. Complete online registration on MIS Web. Card production is triggered by a completed
   registration, so an unregistered student cannot be issued one.
2. Attend the biometric capture session arranged for your college during orientation
   week. Bring your admission letter and a national identity document.
3. Collect the card from the issuing point announced for your college, normally within
   three weeks of capture.

### Replacing a lost or damaged card

1. Report the loss and obtain a police statement where the card was stolen.
2. Pay the replacement charge at the Cash Office and keep the receipt.
3. Submit the receipt, a copy of the police statement where applicable, and a completed
   replacement request form at the ID card office.
4. Collect the replacement card, normally within two weeks.

### Replacement charge

An illustrative replacement charge of GH₵50 is used in this prototype. This figure was
chosen by the project team as a placeholder and is not an official University of Ghana
fee. Confirm the current amount at the Cash Office.

### If your card is withheld or blocked

A card may be deactivated where there are outstanding fees or an unresolved disciplinary
matter. Clear the underlying issue with the relevant office first; reactivation is not
handled at the ID card office.
