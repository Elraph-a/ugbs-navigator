### Deferment of studies

Deferment allows a student to interrupt their programme for a defined period, normally
one semester or one academic year, and resume afterwards without reapplying for
admission. It is distinct from withdrawal, which ends studentship.

### Who may apply for deferment

A student in good standing may apply. Common grounds are documented ill health, financial
hardship, bereavement, or another serious personal circumstance. A student under
investigation for an examination offence is normally not permitted to defer until the
matter is concluded.

### Steps to apply for deferment

1. Obtain a deferment application form from the Academic Affairs Directorate, or from
   your department's administrative office.
2. Complete the form, stating the grounds and the exact period you wish to defer.
3. Attach supporting documents. Medical grounds require a report from the University
   Hospital or a recognised medical facility.
4. Have the form endorsed by your Head of Department.
5. Submit the completed form to the Academic Affairs Directorate for a decision.
6. Collect the written approval and keep it. You will need it to resume.

### Timing

Apply before the published registration deadline for the semester you intend to defer.
An application made after teaching has begun may be treated as a withdrawal rather than a
deferment. A decision is normally communicated within four weeks of submission.

### Fees and deferment

A student who defers before registering for the semester is not normally liable for that
semester's academic fees. A student who has already registered and paid should ask the
Finance Directorate in writing whether the amount can be carried forward, as this is not
automatic. Deferment does not cancel arrears from an earlier semester.

### Resuming after deferment

Report to the Academic Affairs Directorate with your approval letter before the start of
the semester in which you intend to resume, and complete registration in the normal way.
A student who does not resume at the end of an approved deferment period may be deemed to
have withdrawn.
