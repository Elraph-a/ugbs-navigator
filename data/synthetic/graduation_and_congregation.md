### Graduating from the University

A student graduates when the Academic Board has confirmed that all programme requirements
are met. Graduation is the academic act; congregation is the ceremony at which the
certificate is presented.

### Requirements for graduation

You must have passed all core and elective courses required by your programme, accumulated
at least the minimum number of credits set out for your degree in the College handbook,
met any project, internship or long-essay requirement, and cleared all financial
obligations to the University. A student with outstanding fees may be excluded from the
congregation list even where the academic requirements are met.

### Confirming your status before congregation

1. Check your academic record on MIS Web at the end of your final semester and confirm no
   grade is missing or marked incomplete.
2. Where a grade is missing, raise it with your department immediately — a correction runs
   through the change of grade process and takes time.
3. Obtain financial clearance from the Finance Directorate.
4. Complete library and hall clearance.
5. Confirm that your name appears on the provisional graduation list published by your
   department.

### The congregation ceremony

Congregation is normally held once a year. Dates are announced by the University and
published in the academic calendar. Graduands are notified of gown collection, rehearsal
and seating arrangements through their departments.

### Certificates and attestation

Certificates are presented at congregation. A graduand who cannot attend may collect their
certificate afterwards from the Academic Affairs Directorate on production of a student
identity card or other valid identification. Where a certificate is needed before
congregation — for employment or further study — request an attestation letter and an
official transcript from the Academic Affairs Directorate instead.
